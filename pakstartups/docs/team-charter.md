# PakStartups Team Charter & Governance Agreement

**Version:** 2.0 (Revised)  
**Date:** July 26, 2026  
**Status:** Official Active Charter  
**Applies To:** Permanent Team Members & Core Maintainers  

---

## 1. Mission & Vision

### Our Mission
To build the definitive digital operating system for Pakistan's startup ecosystem—democratizing access to vetted company data, co-founder talent, B2B perks, and actionable legal and business tools.

### Our Vision
To position Pakistan as a global innovation powerhouse where every high-potential founder can launch, fund, and scale a venture regardless of background or initial location.

---

## 2. Core Values & Principles

1. **Impact First:** We measure success by the growth, funding, and resilience of Pakistani founders.
2. **Uncompromising Quality & Security:** Data accuracy, security best practices, and user privacy take precedence over rapid shortcuts.
3. **Open Collaboration, Managed Standards:** High-trust internal collaboration with clear code reviews and architecture gates.
4. **Resilience & Continuity:** Clear documentation, clean code standards, and responsible handover procedures.

---

- **Technical Lead & Architect (Muhammad Taha):** Owns system architecture, codebase security, AI integrations, database models, and technical release quality.
- **Administration & Operations Lead:** Owns ecosystem partnerships, community relations, volunteer coordination, and operational administration.

---

## 4. Member Benefits & Expectations (Prioritized)

1. **Ecosystem Leadership & Direct Impact:** Direct influence over platform architecture serving thousands of Pakistani founders and investors.
2. **Technical & Architectural Mastery:** Hands-on experience with modern full-stack web architectures, serverless Firestore pipelines, and AI recommendation engines.
3. **High-Level Network Access:** Direct exposure to top Pakistani founders, VCs, incubators, and startup leaders.
4. **Platform Ownership & Governance:** Strategic voice in product roadmaps and architecture decision records.
5. **Credential & Role Recognition:** Verification of leadership roles for professional profiles (LinkedIn/portfolio) upon meeting performance thresholds.

---

## 5. Security, IP & Offboarding Rules

- **Code & Security Access:** All code remains closed-source in the private repository. Access is granted under least-privilege principles.
- **Intellectual Property:** All contributions belong to PakStartups platform IP.
- **Offboarding & Handover:** Offboarding members must return access credentials, complete code reviews for pending PRs, and update relevant documentation.
